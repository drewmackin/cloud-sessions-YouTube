## 2. Workflow from zero to a posted video (assembled from these videos)

These steps are assembled from several videos. Kevin never presents them as a single list in the returned text.

1. **Mindset first: concept and research beat the AI.** See section 4. Kevin, [WVz 1:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WVzIUxovPdA&t=101s), **VERBATIM**: “AI alone does not make money.”
2. **Find a product.**
   - Pick a TikTok Shop product that already sells. Kevin, [T6 39:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T6-LXSu0qHw&t=2384s), **VERBATIM**: “products that are already doing sales on Tik Tok shop where demand is validated before you post a single video, right?”
   - Or revive an old winner. Kevin, [T6 35:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T6-LXSu0qHw&t=2143s), **VERBATIM**: “products that printed money, you know, years ago or months ago that died with their videos and not their demand.”
   - Or take a competitor's proven product to a gap (see section 7).
   - Keep researching even while making money. Kevin, [0Ew 8:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0EwzL3Etidg&t=506s), **VERBATIM**: “Also, you want to be doing product research consistently and not slacking on research even if you're currently making money.”
3. **Get a supplier and accounts.** Kevin, [0Ew 15:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0EwzL3Etidg&t=912s), **VERBATIM**: “Uh you need to go ahead and find a private supplier and you need to go ahead and start making the accounts to start posting.” For priming accounts, see section 7.
4. **Niche the avatar to the buyer.** See section 5. Kevin, [T6 9:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T6-LXSu0qHw&t=549s), **VERBATIM**: “Make your avatar super niche to the product that you're selling and then just do something controversial, right?”
5. **Build the video.** There are three routes.
   - **(a) Hybrid, Kevin's own method.** Rip the competitor's or affiliates' showcase clips, background-swap them with Seedance, and make only the hook with AI. Kevin, [T6 29:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T6-LXSu0qHw&t=1778s), **VERBATIM**: “I'm just going to rip it, background swap it, and really just take care of the the the hook myself, right? So, the first 3 to 4 seconds of the video, um, I'll take care of it with like a run two or a 321 AI concept.”
   - **(b) 100% AI.** Kevin, [FFP 0:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FFPQYyPqQFI&t=56s), **VERBATIM**: “But you also have, right, the ability to make 100% AI videos and go just as viral even more, okay?”
   - **(c) Beginners: mix AI with your own content.** Kevin, [FFP 0:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FFPQYyPqQFI&t=45s), **VERBATIM**: “Like I mentioned before, you have the ability to either mix AI with our content, right? Which is totally doable, and it's what I kind of recommend for beginners if you're not that good at making AI renderings just just yet.”
6. **Edit.** Combine the AI hook and the real showcase clips in CapCut (see the CapCut row in section 1).
7. **Post.**
   - On volume, Kevin, [0Ew 15:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0EwzL3Etidg&t=929s), **VERBATIM**: “process is going to be actually making and editing the videos, posting three to five videos daily and testing multiple concepts to find, you know, our outlier, right?”
   - On platform order, group call, speaker not labeled, [b3x 47:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b3xKO_MABXg&t=2871s), **VERBATIM**: “on TikTok first, then you repurpose on IG, then Facebook.”
   - In T6, by contrast, the TikTok Shop rips are posted straight to Instagram and Facebook ([T6 40:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T6-LXSu0qHw&t=2441s)).
8. **Read the output and decide.**
   - Kevin, [0Ew 15:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0EwzL3Etidg&t=941s), **VERBATIM**: “And then the output is going to be all right, did this video actually go viral? Did we see any sort of demand? Did we even get sales? And what was the conversion rate on this product?”
   - On the cutoff, [0Ew 17:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0EwzL3Etidg&t=1053s), **VERBATIM**: “you want to be running products for a minimum of 5 days, but max 7 days to really get the ball moving and just cut your losses on the ones that really aren't going to get you a high ROI, right?”
9. **Find the outlier and split test it.** See section 7 (b3x).
10. **Daily routine.**
    - Kevin, [0Ew 20:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0EwzL3Etidg&t=1224s), **VERBATIM**: “let's say in the morning for 3 to four hours, you're filming and you're editing your videos, aiming for six to nine videos a day, right?”
    - Then the afternoon is “doing some PR concept research. Also, you know, posting content and engaging to uh or replying to some comments and DMs, right?” (**VERBATIM**, [0Ew 20:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0EwzL3Etidg&t=1235s)).
    - The evening is store operations, and at night “for 15 minutes, you're reviewing your stores and planning uh tomorrow tonight essentially, right?” (**VERBATIM**, [0Ew 20:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0EwzL3Etidg&t=1251s)).
