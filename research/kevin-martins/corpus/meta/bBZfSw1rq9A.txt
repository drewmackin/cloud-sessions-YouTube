When writing prompts, try to describe these key elements:
📌 Subject: Who or what appears in the video?
📌 Action: What is happening in the scene?
📌 Environment: Where does the scene take place?
📌 Camera Movement: Should the camera zoom in, pan, track, or move around the subject?
📌 Lighting: Is the scene bright, dramatic, soft, or cinematic?
📌 Visual Style: Do you want a realistic, cinematic, documentary, or artistic look?
