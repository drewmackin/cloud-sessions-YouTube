Higgsfield works as a plugin inside ChatGPT, so you can make AI images and videos without switching apps.
You connect it once, and ChatGPT can use the models in Higgsfield's creative suite, including Seedance 2.5, Kling 3, Nano Banana Pro and GPT Image 2.
Steps: add Higgsfield from the ChatGPT Plugins Directory and sign in once.
Then attach a product photo, ask for one carousel image and one short clip, and name the style in a single line.
In the demo, the photo is a candle.
The answer is a carousel image of the candle in warm evening light, plus a short clip in the same style.
Keep the style line in the same prompt as the request.
That is what makes the image and the video match.
