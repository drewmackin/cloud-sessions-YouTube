📊 Brandable Product Research Rubric by Kevin Martins (MAKE A COPY TO EDIT)
Use this rubric to score potential brandable, long-term e‑commerce products on a 1–5 scale in each category (5 = strong, 1 = weak).
Total Possible Points: 45
Goal: Identify products with longevity, demand, strong margins, and viral organic content potential.
________________


Scoring Table
Category
	Score (1–5)
	Detailed Criteria
	Longevity & Variations
	

	The product must have long-term potential, not just a trend. Ideally supports 5+ variations (colors, styles, bundles, editions) and future drops without changing the core idea.
	Wow Factor
	

	The product should look cool, stand out visually, or feel unique. It must grab attention within the first few seconds of content.
	Adds Value
	

	Does the product solve a problem, create happiness, confidence, nostalgia, or entertainment? Value can be functional or emotional, but it must be clear.
	Market Demand
	

	Validate real demand. Are people already buying similar products? Do communities, creators, or audiences exist around this niche? Don’t rely on personal taste.
	Product–Market Fit
	

	Does it make sense for this audience to buy this product? It should feel natural, not forced, within the niche or lifestyle.
	Emotional / Passion Connection
	

	Does it trigger emotion—happiness, nostalgia, excitement, confidence, identity, or belonging? Emotional products convert easier and brand stronger.
	Content Potential
	

	You should instantly think of at least 5 strong content angles (Contrast, POVs, storytelling, 123, public concepts, stitch). If ideas don’t flow, virality will be hard.
	Sharability
	

	Is this something someone would send to a friend, tag someone in, or comment on? High shareability = higher organic reach.
	Giftable
	

	Would someone buy this for another person? Giftable products are easier to scale and have higher intent (birthdays, holidays, special moments).
	Perceived Value & Margins
	

	Must feel premium enough to support 50%+ profit margins through branding, packaging, or positioning. Avoid very small items. (unless jewelry)
	________________


Compliance Check (Pass / Fail — Mandatory)
If any of the following are NO, the product is automatically disqualified:
* ☐ No copyright or trademark issues
* ☐ Not electronics
* ☐ Not a dupe of popular phones, cameras, or tech products
* ☐ Brand‑safe and legally scalable
Compliance Result: PASS / FAIL

🔢 Total Score Guide
* 40–45 = 🟢 Elite Brandable Winner – Greenlight immediately
* 35–39 = 🔥 Strong Winner – Test & validate with content
* 30–34 = 💰 Viable – Needs strong positioning
* 25–29 = 🤔 Mid – Only test with a unique angle
* <25 = ❌ Do Not Test
