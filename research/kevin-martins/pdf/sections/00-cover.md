<div class="note" style="margin-top:24px">
<b>Bottom line.</b> The machine is: <b>Claude writes every prompt → Higgsfield renders</b> (GPT Image 2 for stills, Kling 3.0 for motion, Seedance for background swaps) <b>→ CapCut assembles → 3–6 posts a day</b> across Facebook, Instagram and TikTok. The AI is not the edge. The edge is (1) a standing <b>context prompt</b> plus coach-written prompt docs, (2) a <b>niched avatar</b> built from customer research, (3) <b>proven concepts</b> (“run two”, “321”) copied and re-skinned, and (4) brute-force <b>volume and split testing</b>. Inside the Discord, a weekly loop runs it: about five group calls a week, product sheets that Kevin approves, prompts handed out on calls, and a wins chat that doubles as the sales pitch. Shared products and prompts saturate within days, so speed after a “leak” matters more than the prompt itself.
</div>
