## The core belief: AI is a multiplier, not the product

> {{q:GfhpQLqF658|I would say that this business model is 80% mindset|AI doesn't work if you don't know what organic marketing is}}

> {{q:WVzIUxovPdA|AI alone does not make money.|from people doing real numbers,}}

**Our read:** they treat AI as a way to make content cheaply in volume, and nothing more. What makes money is still the product, the concept and the targeting. That's why the same people who preach “100% AI” spend most of their screen time on product research and copying proven concepts.

## Seven principles they repeat

**1. Stay emotionless; decide on data.**
{{q:GfhpQLqF658|organic is one of those business models where you have to stay emotionless.|through both the highs and the lows,}}
{{q:GfhpQLqF658|Remember guys, the numbers never lie.|The market never lies.}}

**2. First-principles thinking over copying blindly**, while still copying what's proven to test it.
{{q:GfhpQLqF658|first principal thinking is the process of breaking a problem|relying on assumptions.}}
{{q:b3xKO_MABXg|So again, we're just copying to test.|don't reinvent the wheel.}}

**3. Demand beats virality. Views are not money.**
{{q:GfhpQLqF658|Is there actually demand behind this virality?|Do you want to make money or do you want to get views?}}
{{q:WVzIUxovPdA|hype doesn't necessarily convert to money.|hype doesn't necessarily convert to money.}}

**4. Products aren't saturated; angles are.**
{{q:T6-LXSu0qHw|products aren't saturated.|It's typically just the marketing angle.}}

**5. Speed and volume win.**
{{q:b3xKO_MABXg|When you got guys posting four to six videos a day,|you get data back much quicker,}}
{{q:0EwzL3Etidg|test, learn, and iterate,|the faster you can find a winner.}}

**6. Give a product a fair test, then cut it without regret.**
{{q:GfhpQLqF658|sometimes you genuinely need to give a product time|30 to 40 posts.}}
{{q:0EwzL3Etidg|running products for a minimum of 5 days,|just cut your losses}}

**7. Treat AI as leverage, and ignore the people who say it doesn't work.**
{{q:0EwzL3Etidg|leverage is using AI platforms and systems|effort alone ever could.}}
{{q:GfhpQLqF658|Obviously, AI does work with organic.|Of course, traditional organic still works.}}

<div class="note"><b>The non-obvious part.</b> The mindset they sell (emotionless, data-driven, high volume) exists because the work turns into a numbers game. When one video costs a few credits and 20 minutes, the winners are the ones who can face 30–40 flops per product without quitting. The people who fail are described as the ones who “lost conviction” after a few posts (Kevin's breakdown of a 6.6M-view creator who gave up, May 19 video). Their real edge is a high tolerance for failure plus fast iteration.</div>
