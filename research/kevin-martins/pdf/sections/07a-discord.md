## Still locked, and how to get it

- **Not public:** the full channel list beyond the wins chat, the pinned prompts (Elias's 321 prompt, the run-two prompts, Kevin's full context prompt), the FSE app, unpublished call recordings and day-to-day member chat.
- **If you are a member**, two ways to add it to this report with the same proof standard:
    - Run a Claude session on your own computer, where your Discord login is (the Claude desktop app, or `claude remote-control` in a terminal), and have it read the channels you can see.
    - Export the channels with DiscordChatExporter and put the export in your Google Drive. The toolkit in your repo (`tools/discord/`) turns it into a sourced evidence pack. Exporting with your own account token is against Discord's terms and risks that account. A bot added by the server owner is the clean route.
