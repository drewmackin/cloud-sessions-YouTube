## What they stopped using, and why it matters

{{q:hK2Os3wSPI8|back in the day with Sora 2,|just not believable, right?}}

The model churn is fast. A year earlier the options were “Sora 2 and Vio”. By mid-2026 the stack was Higgsfield, GPT Image 2, Kling 3.0 and Seedance, and the deciding feature was **character consistency** from a single master frame. Expect the model names to change again. The structure (research → context prompt → master frame → stills → motion → real-clip edit) is what has stayed the same.
